Scratch the foil, wake the bunny

Rub the card and the silver comes away under your cursor. Underneath is a small kindness — nothing grand, just the sort of thing you would want to hear on an ordinary afternoon. Fourteen of them, plus three golden ones that turn up rarely.

The bunny watches your pointer while you work. Clear enough of the card and it reveals itself with confetti; catch a shooting star as it crosses and your luck goes up by one. Cards scratched, golden cards found and luck earned are all remembered in your browser between visits — nothing leaves your machine.

Scratching

The foil is a real canvas mask — every stroke erases where you actually dragged, and the reveal fires once enough of it is gone.

Golden cards

Roughly every third card is golden by default, with a bigger blessing and a louder celebration. The rest are the everyday kind.

Kept for you

Your card number, streak and luck live in local storage. No account, no server, no analytics — clear your browser data and the bunny forgets.
